## What HiTHIUM sells

- [Cells](https://www.hithium.com/products/cell.html): large-format prismatic LFP energy-storage cells
- [Modules](https://www.hithium.com/products/module.html): battery modules built from those cells
- [Utility systems](https://www.hithium.com/products/system.html): liquid-cooled containerised BESS; published certifications UL 1973, UL 9540, UL 9540A, NFPA 855, IEC 62619, IEC 62477, IEC 63056, IEC 61000, UN 38.3
- [C&I systems](https://www.hithium.com/products/commercial.html): commercial and industrial storage
- [Residential systems](https://www.hithium.com/products/residential.html): home storage, marketed as HeroEE at https://www.hero-ee.com/

## Integration reality

- There is no web API. Third-party integration with HiTHIUM BESS is over industrial fieldbus
  interfaces (Modbus/CAN between BMS, PCS and EMS), described in product datasheets and manuals.
- Those datasheets and manuals sit behind an account gate: the download centre answers
  "Please login to download this datasheet" (https://www.hithium.com/support/download_center.html).
- The HeroEE residential/portable range has a consumer mobile app; its backend is undocumented and
  no public API is offered for it.
- Manuals for the HeroEE line are hosted on a third-party Feishu/Lark workspace, not on a
  HiTHIUM-controlled documentation host.

## Security

- [Cybersecurity Bulletin / PSIRT](https://www.hithium.com/support/security.html): HiTHIUM operates a
  Product Security Incident Response Team running an IEC 62443-aligned vulnerability management
  process. Report vulnerabilities to IACS-CyberSecurity@hithium.com.
- Two advisories published to date: HESSCS-25090001 (ICMP timestamp disclosure, CVE-1999-0524,
  2025-09-25) and HESSCS-25090002 (Dropbear information disclosure, CVE-2019-12953, 2025-09-22),
  both against the ∞Block 5.016MWh / 4.180MWh systems.
